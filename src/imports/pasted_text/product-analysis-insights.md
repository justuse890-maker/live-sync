After analyzing these products and the user feedback patterns, I would **not copy them directly**.

The biggest insight is:

> Users are not asking for more finance features.
> Users are asking for less friction.

This appears repeatedly across MonAI, Say, ExpenseAI, Reddit discussions, and broader AI-app review research. Users praise:

* Voice entry
* Automatic capture
* SMS detection
* Receipt scanning
* Simple UI
* Fast onboarding

Users complain about:

* Too much manual entry
* Complex dashboards
* Too many screens
* Subscription pricing without clear value
* Privacy concerns
* AI that feels generic instead of personal ([MonAi][1])
